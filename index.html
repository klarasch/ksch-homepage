01 / About

Working at the intersection of cybersecurity, AI and enterprise software.

Background

My background combines computer science, new media studies, music education, interaction design and visual communication. I work on complex systems used by technical users — from platform tooling and AI SIEM workflows to IDE-like environments and scalable design systems.

I care about products being understandable, buildable and commercially useful. I prefer informed iteration over process theatre, and prototypes over abstract alignment decks.

I also occasionally contribute implementation improvements directly and submit pull requests when it makes sense — instead of pretending design stops at handoff.
